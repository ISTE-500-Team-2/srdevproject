## Substitutions (design MPN not orderable in quantity)

- C3 C4 C6: design C0805C226M8PACTU -> fitted Samsung Electro-Mechanics CL21A226MAQNNNE (C45783). 22uF 25V X5R 20% 0805. Higher voltage rating than the 10V KEMET, so DC-bias derating at 3.3-5 V is no worse (judgment - the v0.3 power review used the KEMET bias curve; re-check effective C if margins were tight). JLC basic part.
- R44: design CRCW08054R70FKEAHP -> fitted ROHM Semiconductor ESR10EZPF4R70 (C307921). 4.7 ohm 1% 0.4 W anti-surge 0805. Design note for R44 accepts any 0805 >= 0.25 W; worst-case stuck-low dissipation is 0.19 W.
- SW1 SW2: design TL3701AF100QG -> fitted HYP (Hongyuan Precision) 1TS016A-1000-0600-CT (C390633). 3.0x2.6 mm 4-lead tact, lead span 3.5 (TL3701 3.6), row pitch 1.8, height 0.60 (TL3701 0.65), 100 gf, travel 0.15, 12 V/50 mA, 300k cycles. Circuit: 1-2 common, 3-4 common, switch between the two rows - same as TL3701, so the board's row-wise nets do not short. HYP land 0.55x0.6 at (+-1.475, +-0.9) sits inside the board's 0.75x0.8 pads at (+-1.425, +-0.9).
- U1: design ESP32-C3-MINI-1-N4X -> fitted Espressif Systems ESP32-C3-MINI-1-H4X (C41349510). Same module outline/pinout and same 'X' silicon revision as the designed N4X; 4 MB flash; 105 C rated instead of 85 C. Firmware-identical (judgment from Espressif ordering code, not bench-tested).
