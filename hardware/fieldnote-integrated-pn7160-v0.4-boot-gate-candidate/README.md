## Circuit and operating settings

U8 is TI SN74LVC1G97DBVR. In1 is grounded; In0 receives ESP_RESET_RC; In2 receives POWER_PERMIT. Its Schmitt output ESP_EN drives only U1 pin 8 CHIP_EN and a 47 kΩ R29 pulldown. R12, C9, SW1 and J4 pin 5 remain on the reset input, so manual/external reset does not short the push-pull gate output. R12's high side now receives POWER_PERMIT. R30 is also 47 kΩ to bound both input leakages when the selector is open. C38 is the local 100 nF gate supply capacitor. Existing R2 changes from 100 kΩ to exact 10 kΩ RC0603FR-0710KL; its net and physical land pattern are unchanged.

JP1 is a PCB-only mutually exclusive selector, excluded from both BOM and placement exports. The default copper bridge joins pad 1 USB_HIGH_CURRENT to pad 2 POWER_PERMIT. Normal full operation requires the accepted USB-C supply advertising at least 1.5 A. To select experimental SERVICE, completely cut the pad 1–2 copper bridge before soldering pad 2–3 to 3V3. Never bridge both sides: that would connect the U7 detector's push-pull output to 3V3. Modify the selector while unpowered and verify the open/correct connections before applying power.

SERVICE provides an explicit gate bypass for controlled bring-up and future battery investigation. Reliable boot on a legacy low-current USB port is not established. No battery is fitted now. Normal operation still requires USB; future battery operation requires explicit service selection plus separate charge, protection, transient-load and PN7160 voltage-aware TXLDO qualification.

## Electrical limitations that retain HOLD

The primary-source calculation in [references/usb-startup-sequence-audit-v2](references/usb-startup-sequence-audit-v2/) screens exact 3 V steady logic levels and a conditional empty-C9 cold step. Conservative leakage gives a USB_HIGH_CURRENT low bound 0.3048 V below BQ's 0.4 V low limit. An unbridged 47 kΩ permission pulldown bounds both gate input leakages to 0.47755 V below the Schmitt falling threshold minimum 0.84 V. The RC input high bound 2.1612 V exceeds the rising threshold maximum 1.87 V. These are exact-voltage bounds, not arbitrary-ramp results.

An empty C9 with the bounded valid3 V permission step gives 11.545–121.081 ms delay before reset release. C9 can retain charge after a brief permission loss, so the next permission edge may give no fresh delay. Direct permission input asserts reset at valid logic levels, but no guaranteed BQ EN2 mode-transition settling time is established. Ioff limits only cover the stated supply condition; off/ramp/replug operation and the full converter startup transient remain unqualified. The prior full startup simulation issue is not fixed by adding a logic gate.

The retained ESP_EN output route is 54.1377 mm with two vias: 3.6890 mm front copper and 50.4488 mm back copper. A read-only search rejected the closer underside sites because they lie in preserved Wi-Fi track/via exclusions. A second reroute saved only 0.9794 mm while adding two vias; it was not adopted. [EN-reference-screen.json](output/native-review-candidate-v4a/EN-reference-screen.json) samples saved ground polygons at ≤0.025 mm along the centerline and both track edges. Centerline adjacent-ground inclusion is 113/153 samples on In1 below front routing and 1222/2033 samples on In2 above back routing; full-width counts are lower. This route cannot be described as continuously referenced to an adjacent ground plane. The screen is geometric only. Gate/trace capacitance, ringing, overshoot, noise pickup, return impedance and electromagnetic coupling have not been extracted or physically checked. No series resistor or source keepout change was silently introduced.

The remaining acceptance work is the independent exact-package/case/assembly/RF/firmware review, shared power/reset transient diagnosis, manufacturer file/parts/process confirmation, and physical startup/reset/NFC/Wi-Fi testing on assembled boards.
